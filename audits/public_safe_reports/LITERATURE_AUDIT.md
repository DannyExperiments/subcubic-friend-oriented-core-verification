# Literature and priority for Q236

Search cutoff 26 November 2026. The original question is in [Simola, dissertation, §5.5](https://doi.org/10.34726/hss.2026.138201).

No prior resolution of the three-mutual-friend case was located. Confidence in the bounded priority search is moderate to high.

The AAMAS proceedings define degree using the union of neighbourhoods but their symmetric bounds use both arcs of each mutual friendship. Appendix A.4 of arXiv v2 treats paths and cycles (two mutual friends); Appendix A.2 uses four mutual friends in the hard construction. Simola’s thesis Proposition 5.2(iii), Theorem 5.4 and §5.5 make the threshold gap explicit for undirected graphs. The asymmetric degree-three and planar degree-four hardness results have different hypotheses. Proceedings and thesis forward citations were also searched.
