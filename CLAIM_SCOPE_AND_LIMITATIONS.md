# Scope and limitations

Both partition verification problems are coNP-complete for symmetric friend-oriented hedonic games without neutrals and with at most three mutual friends. With at most two they are polynomial-time decidable.

The input includes the partition. Nonedges represent mutual enemies. The reduction permits nonplanar graphs. The threshold concerns the maximum number of distinct friends; degree-two tractability is a prior theorem.

No prior resolution of the three-mutual-friend case was located. Confidence in the bounded priority search is moderate to high.
